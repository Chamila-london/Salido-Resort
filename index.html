Welcome

A small resort,
run properly

Salido Resort sits on Riverview Road in Tennekumbura — close enough to Kandy to do everything, far enough out to sleep well. Rooms are simple, clean and comfortable, air-conditioned or not, as you prefer.

Most of our guests come to see Kandy: the Temple of the Tooth, the lake, the Hantana hills. We're the quiet part of that trip — a place to come back to at the end of the day.

    Getting to Kandy

    We're about 3 hours from Colombo. Most guests come by train — the scenic way — or by bus. Here's how, plus what the weather's doing right now.

    Loading Kandy weather…

    By train from Colombo

    The Colombo Fort → Kandy line is one of Asia's most scenic rides — 116 km through tea country, about 2½–3 hours. Sit on the right for the best views.

    • 05:55 Podi Menike scenic blue train
    • 07:00 Intercity Express fastest, ~2h25m
    • 08:30 Udarata Menike scenic blue train
    • 15:35 Intercity fast afternoon
    • 17:45 last train evening

    Fares from ~LKR 350 (2nd class). Reserved seats sell out fast — book ahead at railway.gov.lk. Times can change, so please check before you travel.

    By bus from Colombo

    Intercity buses leave Colombo (Pettah / Bastian Mawatha stand) for Kandy very frequently — roughly every 15–30 minutes, no booking needed. The trip takes about 3–4 hours depending on traffic.

    • AC intercity comfiest, ~LKR 500–700
    • Normal bus cheapest, ~LKR 250–350

    Buses run from early morning until late evening. A taxi or private car is ~3 hours via the expressway — we're happy to help arrange a pickup, just message us on WhatsApp.
